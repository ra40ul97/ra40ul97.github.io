About me

I’m Raoul, Director of Photography and Director based in Milan.

I come from economics and marketing, until I realised I’d rather be behind a camera than behind a desk. I learned in the field, between sets, courses and a lot of curiosity.

I bring cinematography and direction together: I step into the director’s vision, help shape it from the storyboard onwards and put every image at the service of the story. My style adapts to each project, but symmetry, sharpness and contrast are a bit of a weakness of mine.

Available worldwide: wherever there’s a good story to tell.
